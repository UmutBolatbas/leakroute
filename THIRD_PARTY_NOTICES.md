## Detection rules

The secret patterns in `src/rules/secrets/patterns.ts` were written from the
providers' public documentation of their token formats. They are not copied
from other secret scanners. In particular, no code or rules come from
copyleft-licensed scanners (such as AGPL-3.0 projects) or from rule sets under
non-open-source licenses.
